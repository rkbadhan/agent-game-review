Reading a review

Four ideas carry the whole surface. You can reopen them any time from Glossary in the sidebar.

Review mode
Whether this run got only mechanically derived facts (Deterministic), added AI interpretation (AI-enriched), or too little was captured to review at all (Not reviewable).
Evidence grade
How well a moment's facts are grounded — Strong, Moderate, or Limited — based on how complete the capture was.
Cause (attribution)
How far a cause is actually established: a linked event is on the evidence path, not proven to have changed the outcome. Only replay can claim that.
Fact vs. interpretation
Each moment block names its own source. Validated facts and source events are observed; likely impact and better actions are interpretation, and a deterministic review leaves them blank rather than inventing them.
